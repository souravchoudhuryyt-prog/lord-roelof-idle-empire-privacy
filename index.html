Privacy Policy

Lord Roelof's Idle Empire

Last updated: 17 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Lord Roelof's Idle Empire, developed and published by The Roelof Studio / Roelof Johnson, handles information when you use the game.

1. Information You Provide

The game does not require you to create an account and does not ask you to provide your name, postal address, phone number, contacts, photographs, precise location, or payment-card details in order to play.

2. Local Game Data

The game stores gameplay information locally on your device so your progress can be restored when you return. This may include business progress, upgrades, robots, settings, tutorial progress, audio preferences, offline progress, and other game-state information.

You can erase locally stored gameplay data using the Reset Save Data option inside the game. Resetting save data is permanent and cannot be undone.

3. Optional Rewarded Advertising

Lord Roelof's Idle Empire may offer optional rewarded advertisements. Core gameplay is not intended to require watching an advertisement. Rewarded ads are used only when you choose to request an advertising reward, such as a temporary gameplay boost or an enhanced offline reward.

The production version of the game uses Unity LevelPlay for rewarded-ad delivery and mediation, with configured advertising demand that includes Google AdMob and Unity/ironSource demand. These services may process technical and advertising-related information required to provide, measure, secure, limit, and prevent fraud in advertisements. Depending on the provider, region, device settings, and consent choices, this may include device or advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location derived from IP, device and app information, app or ad interactions, diagnostics, and fraud-prevention signals.

The game asks for an in-game rewarded-ad privacy choice before initializing LevelPlay. If you decline, you can continue playing without rewarded ads. In regions where law or advertising-platform policy requires an additional certified consent-management flow, that regional consent flow must also be completed before eligible ad processing.

Advertising providers process information under their own privacy policies and applicable law. Unity's privacy policy is available at unity.com/legal/privacy-policy. Google's privacy policy is available at policies.google.com/privacy.

4. Payments and App Store Services

If purchases are introduced or made available through Google Play or another application marketplace, payment processing is handled by that platform. The developer does not directly receive or store your full payment-card or banking information.

Google Play and other distribution platforms may independently process information related to installation, updates, licensing, purchases, crash reporting, device compatibility, and platform security according to their own terms and privacy policies.

5. External Links

The game may contain buttons that open external websites or applications. For example, the Contact Roelof Johnson button may open the developer's Facebook page.

Once you leave the game, the external service is governed by its own terms and privacy policy. The developer is not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party websites or applications.

6. Analytics, Diagnostics and Future Services

The developer may use platform-provided diagnostics, crash information, or other technical information needed to maintain and improve the game. If additional analytics, cloud-save, account, advertising, or online services are added in a future version, this Privacy Policy will be updated to reflect those services before or when they are introduced.

7. Data Sharing and Sale

The developer does not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by service providers only as necessary to operate features such as app distribution, rewarded advertising, diagnostics, security, or other services described in this policy.

8. Children

The game is not designed to knowingly request personal information directly from children. Parents and guardians should supervise children's use of online services, external links, advertisements, and application stores in accordance with the age requirements and parental-control options of those services.

9. Security and Retention

Local gameplay information remains on the device until it is overwritten, reset, removed with the application, or otherwise deleted by the operating system. Third-party service providers may retain information for periods described in their own privacy policies and as required for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, or service operation.

10. Your Choices

  • You can play without creating a developer account.
  • You can choose whether to use optional rewarded-ad features.
  • You can reset locally stored game progress from the Settings menu.
  • You can manage advertising and privacy controls made available by your device, Google Play, and advertising providers.
  • You may contact the developer with privacy questions using the email address below.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This policy may be updated when the game, its third-party services, or legal requirements change. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will be changed when a revised policy is published.
